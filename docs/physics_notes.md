Internal notes that don't belong in the manuscript itself: derivations
in progress, alternative formulations, references to chase down, and
open questions. Keep entries dated.

```

**Context:** what part of the manuscript this affects.
**Question / hypothesis:**
**Working notes:**
**Decision / next step:**
```

Move anything that becomes part of the paper into the appropriate file
under `manuscript/sections/` or `manuscript/appendices/`.
