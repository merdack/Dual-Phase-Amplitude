\title{Dual Phase-Amplitude Coupling in Synchronized Scalar Fields:\\
A Dynamical Phase-Stress Approach}

\author{S. Hadi Mahdavi Mortazavi}
\email{kamerdack@gmail.com}
\affiliation{Independent Researcher, Tehran, Iran}

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
A covariant phase-locked Klein--Gordon (PLKG) framework is formulated for $N$ complex scalar fields in a flat Friedmann--Lema\^{\i}tre--Robertson--Walker background.
An $S_N$-symmetric democratic interaction is adopted whose polar decomposition yields Kuramoto-type $\cos(\theta_k-\theta_j)$ and $\sin(\theta_k-\theta_j)$ couplings weighted by amplitudes $\rho_j\rho_k$, rather than a phenomenological oscillator network added by hand.
From the corresponding first-principles Lagrangian, coupled amplitude and phase equations are derived without dropping spatio-temporal derivatives.
The symmetric energy--momentum tensor is obtained by metric variation so that the synchronized sector sources the Friedmann equations explicitly.
In the overdamped homogeneous limit the phase dynamics reduce to a classical Kuramoto equation with Hubble-modified effective coupling $K_{\mathrm{eff}}=K/(3HN)$.
A continuum spectral limit is also exhibited.
An effective stiff-like synchronized regime ($w_{\mathrm{sync}}\approx 1$) is distinguished from the vacuum-like bare interaction potential ($w_{V_{\mathrm{int}}}=-1$).
Linear stability, conservation on shell, and illustrative numerical integrations of the truncated and grid-resolved dynamics are summarized.
Implications for fuzzy dark matter phenomenology, early-universe constraints, and schematic structure-formation diagnostics are outlined.
\end{abstract}
