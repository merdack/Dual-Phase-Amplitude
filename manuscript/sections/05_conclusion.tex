\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

By rigorous derivation of the phase-locked Klein--Gordon (PLKG) field equations from a fundamental Lagrangian formulation, the exact phase-amplitude dynamics were obtained without prior approximations.
The energy-momentum tensor was computed, and it was shown that the phase-synchronization interaction sources the Friedmann equations explicitly, thereby closing the gravitational back-reaction loop.
The model reduces to the classic Kuramoto system in the appropriate homogeneous, overdamped limit (Eqs.~\eqref{eq:kuramoto_limit} and~\eqref{eq:keff}), and a covariant framework for studying relativistic synchronization in cosmology is provided.
